Where we actually are

The risk before launch is not "we need to build features" — it is that most features have never been verified end-to-end in production with real money and real emails. This is a verification-and-readiness plan, not a build plan.

Just unblocked: Google approved the calendar.readonly sensitive scope. Google Calendar OAuth (built July) is now available to all users, not just ≤100 test users — the "connect your calendar, no file upload" flow is the launch hero, pending one live end-to-end check.
The beta uses Google Calendar — it's Plan A and it works. Now the scope is approved and live, the beta cohort connects Google Calendar like any real user will. That's the point of a beta: exercise the primary path before founders arrive. .ics upload still exists as an alternative, but it's not the beta's route and not a fallback we depend on.

06 · Go-to-market

Two audiences, two jobs

They map cleanly onto the two output lanes — and onto two different jobs in the launch. One hardens the product; the other pays for it.

Audience 1 · the real market · revenue

Female founders & solopreneurs

  • Output lane: Finance — Xero / MYOB CSV.
  • The magic: "your calendar becomes your invoices."
  • The A$5–15/mo payers who stay — the product is for them.
  • They need Google Calendar sync — why friction-free launch leans on the approval now in hand.
Audience 2 · not the market · the QA army

Vibe coders + devs

  • Output lane: Jira worklogs.
  • Not revenue — but they'll trial for a free month just to play.
  • "Devs who rip vibe-coded apps apart" = the most robust beta testers.
  • Connect Google Calendar + log to Jira — the same primary path a real user takes.
Vibe coders → break & harden the live product → product proven → founder public launch

Audience 2 de-risks Audience 1. They stress the real, Google-approved path so founders meet a product that's already been through its paces.

Positioning — one story, the founder one

"Your calendar already knows what you worked on. Time Translator turns it into invoices and worklogs — you just review."
Differentiator: AI assists, you approve — never "AI does your books." Park the enterprise pivot (Phase 2).

Pricing (confirm before launch): A$5/mo · 50 — A$15/mo · 150. ⚠ Reconcile against the enforced entitlement limits (200 trial / 5,000 paid) before publishing pricing copy.
